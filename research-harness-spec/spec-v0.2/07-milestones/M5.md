# M5 — Pilot

> Contracts and task list. Full task cards are written in a separate chat before M5 starts. Holdout thresholds apply here.

**Goal.** A test article by the author with the full lab; A/B(/C); critic comparison; thesis-module check on F7; keep / simplify decision.

**Requirements.** NFR-09, NFR-15; T16. Plus pilot items from 02 §1.6 (prompt language) and ADR-010 p.7 (value of the unit-level reviewer).

**Exit criterion (`05-eval-base.md` §8).** All M1–M4 + T16, T47, T48, T53 (threshold), T60 (threshold), T64 (critic comparison); A/B(/C) per §9. Deterministic 100%. Semantic: F4-H **precision and recall of support verdict ≥ 0.9** with CI and n; **MF = 0**; F7: recall of D15, D17 ≥ 0.85 [П]; T60 recall of key works ≥ 0.8 [П]; MY and MU — values with CI.

**Prerequisites.** M4 merged. From the author: budget for the pilot; F6 style samples and dictations; his list of 5 key works for the F4-W sub-topic (postponed from M2, decision Q-M2-3); the target journal of the first case if chosen (journal pack); time for blinded evaluation (3 tasks × 3 repeats × variants); the real first case (article on AI in teaching).

## Pilot design (`05-eval-base.md` §9)

- Variants: **A** — same host and model + short brief, no harness; **B** — A + harness without a separate reviewer; **C** — B + reviewer in a fresh context (only if B works).
- Tasks (3 × 3 repeats): review section on F4-W; response to 5 reviewer comments; conceptual section with definitions.
- Fixed model IDs and date, equal budget and tools, cost logged including failures. The author scores shuffled outputs without variant labels. Metrics MS, MF, MV, MT, M$, MY.
- Decision: B not better than A → simplify the harness to templates, provenance and validators. C not better than B (with cost) → remove the separate unit-level reviewer (the integration critic for theses stays, ADR-010 p.7).
- **Language A/B (02 §1.6):** one task with English vs Ukrainian writer and critic prompts on a Ukrainian manuscript; if the Ukrainian prompts are equal or better on MS, MF, MY → switch prompts to Ukrainian.
- **Thesis readiness (`05-eval-base.md` §9):** full cycle on F7 — thesis intake → structure → context packs for 3 subsections → unit, chapter, integration critics → `.docx` export / import. A mechanism check, not text quality.
- **Real case (T16):** the author's article, full lab; its texts are not added to W or H.
